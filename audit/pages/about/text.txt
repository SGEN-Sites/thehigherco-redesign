ABOUT
Pioneering a New Era in Cannabis

Welcome to The Higher Company, Syracuse’s second downtown licensed New York State dispensary. As one of the first 137 dispensaries to open in the state, we are proud to be part of a historic moment in cannabis legalization. Our mission is to provide high-quality cannabis products and create a welcoming environment for our community.

Our Story

The Higher Company was founded with a vision to transform the cannabis industry and promote its benefits to a wider audience. Our founding members are proud participants in New York’s Cannabis Adult-Use Retail Dispensary (CAURD) program. Having been previously convicted for cannabis-related offenses, they have turned their past experiences into a positive force for change. Their journey from adversity to advocacy is a testament to resilience and the transformative power of cannabis.

“We believe in the power of cannabis to enhance well-being and improve quality of life,” says Joe Sweet. “Our journey began with a passion for the plant and a commitment to providing safe, accessible, and high-quality cannabis products to our community.”

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver the highest quality cannabis products while fostering a sense of community and support. We strive to educate the public, break down stigmas, and advocate for the responsible use of cannabis. At The Higher Company, we are dedicated to:

Quality: Sourcing only the best products from trusted growers and producers.
Variety: Offering a wide range of products to meet diverse needs and preferences.
Education: Providing accurate information and resources to help our customers make informed decisions.
Community: Building a supportive and inclusive community through events, outreach, and advocacy.
Our Products

At The Higher Company, quality is our top priority. We offer a diverse selection of cannabis products, including:

Flower: Premium sativas, indicas, and hybrids.
Edibles: Tasty and effective options, from gummies to baked goods.
Concentrates: Potent and pure extracts like wax, shatter, and oils.
Pre-Rolls: Convenient and ready-to-use pre-rolled joints.
Topicals: Cannabis-infused lotions, balms, and creams for topical use.
Accessories: Everything you need to enhance your cannabis experience.
Community Engagement

We believe in giving back to our community and promoting positive change. Through our Stop the Stigma initiatives, we aim to educate the public about the benefits of cannabis and dispel common misconceptions. Join us at our events, workshops, and rallies to learn more and get involved.
